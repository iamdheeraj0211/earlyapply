How to choose a LinkedIn job search extension

By Dheeraj · Updated Nov 2026

Short answer: pick an extension that works on LinkedIn's own job pages, asks for few permissions, shows you what it hides, and never applies for you. Use the 7-point checklist below, then test it in a clean browser profile. Checked November 2026.

Many extensions say they help with LinkedIn job search. They do very different things, and some ask for more access than they need. Here is a short checklist you can use on any of them, and a 5-minute test.

Extension or job board?

A browser extension works on LinkedIn's own pages. It changes how your LinkedIn search looks, and it has no job listings of its own. A job board is a separate website with its own listings. This guide is about extensions only.

The 7-point checklist

  1. Works on both searches

    LinkedIn has a classic job search and a newer AI search. Check that the extension works on the one you use.

  2. Newest jobs first

    The new AI search can't sort by date. A good extension lists fresh jobs newest first, or hides jobs older than an hour you choose.

  3. Hides the noise, and shows it

    It should hide promoted, reposted and staffing-agency jobs. It should also tell you how many it hid, and let you show them again.

  4. Alerts within minutes

    LinkedIn's own alerts are a daily or weekly email, or an app notification it times itself. Ask how fast the extension's alerts are, and whether they work while you do other things.

  5. Few permissions, no account

    Open the extension's page in the store and read its permissions. Be careful with any extension that wants to read all websites, or asks you to sign in for basic features.

  6. No auto-apply

    LinkedIn's rules prohibit software that automates actions on the site. Choose an extension that only helps you find and filter jobs, and leaves the applying to you.

  7. Free, with a clear privacy page

    You should be able to read in plain words what it stores and where.

Test any extension in 5 minutes

  1. Make a new browser profile

    Use a new Chrome or Edge profile with no other extensions.

  2. Check the permissions

    Install the extension and open its permission list (Manage extensions, then Details).

  3. Run one search

    Open LinkedIn Jobs and run one search. Check that the number of hidden jobs is shown.

  4. Read the privacy page

    Look for what is stored and what is sent out.

  5. Remove it if anything is unclear

    It takes one click.

Questions

Is it safe to use a LinkedIn extension?

It depends on the extension. Check the permissions, read the privacy page, and avoid any that applies for you or asks for your password. LinkedIn's own help page lists the software it does not allow.

Do these extensions work in the LinkedIn app?

No. Extensions run in a browser on a computer, not in the phone app.

What is the difference between an extension and a job board?

An extension works on LinkedIn's own pages and has no job listings of its own. A job board is a separate website with its own listings.
