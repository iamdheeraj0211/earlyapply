Is EarlyApply really free?

Yes. Every feature, including Job Radar, is free. No account, no card.

Will LinkedIn block my account?

EarlyApply only filters and sorts what LinkedIn already shows you, and refreshes your own tab like you would. It never applies, clicks or sends messages for you, and it never scrapes LinkedIn in the background.

Does it apply to jobs for me?

No, and that's on purpose. You read the job and apply yourself. EarlyApply just gets you there first.

How fast are the alerts?

Job Radar checks your search every 3 minutes (you can choose up to 1 hour) and alerts you when it finds a new job.

Does it work in Microsoft Edge?

Yes, it's on the Edge Add-ons store too, and works on LinkedIn's classic and AI-powered job search.
