Quick fixes

I installed EarlyApply. Where is it?

Open LinkedIn Jobs and search for a job title. The EarlyApply bar appears above the job list. It does not show on the LinkedIn feed or on a single job page.

Why do I see no alerts from Job Radar?

Keep your browser open, with the LinkedIn Jobs tab open. On Windows, open Settings, then System, then Notifications, and make sure notifications are on for your browser.

How do I update EarlyApply right away?

Chrome and Edge update it on their own. To check now, open chrome://extensions (Edge: edge://extensions), turn on Developer mode, click Update, then turn Developer mode off again.

Why does the bar say "Nothing to hide on this page"?

EarlyApply checked the jobs on the page and found none that your filters needed to hide.
