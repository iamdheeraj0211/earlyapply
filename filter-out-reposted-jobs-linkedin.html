How to filter out reposted jobs on LinkedIn

By Dheeraj · Updated Nov 2026

Short answer: LinkedIn has no setting or filter for reposted jobs, not even with Premium. You can dismiss them one at a time with the ✕ on each job, or switch on Reposted in the free EarlyApply extension, which hides them all as the page loads.
Read the steps ↓

You search for fresh jobs, and some of them say Reposted. The company posted the job again, so it looks new, but it may already be weeks old with a long line of applicants. LinkedIn gives you no way to turn them off. Here is how to spot them, dismiss them by hand, and hide them automatically.

How to spot a reposted job

  • The job card says Reposted X days ago instead of X days ago.
  • The job can look brand new, but the applicant count is often already high.
  • It was usually posted again because the position is still open.

By hand, on LinkedIn

  1. Look for the word "Reposted"

    Check each card before you open it. It sits where the posted time is shown.

  2. Dismiss it with the ✕

    Click the ✕ in the corner of the card. LinkedIn hides that job from your searches. It works, but one job at a time, and new reposts keep coming.

  3. Keep your search recent

    Pick Past 24 hours in Date posted. Logged in, LinkedIn treats anything shorter as 24 hours and has no "Most recent" sort, and reposted jobs can still appear. For a shorter window, see how to see LinkedIn jobs posted in the last hour.

Automatically, with EarlyApply

Open LinkedIn Jobs and look at the Hide: row of the EarlyApply bar. Switch on Reposted. Every reposted job is hidden, on every search, in LinkedIn's classic and AI-powered search. A line like "5 hidden · show" lets you peek at what was removed.

It also hides Promoted jobs and staffing agencies: see how to remove promoted jobs on LinkedIn.

Why it's worth hiding them

If you want to be one of the first applicants, a reposted job is a bad bet: it has been open for a while and the line is already long. Hiding them leaves more room for jobs that were really posted minutes ago.

Questions

Can LinkedIn hide reposted jobs?

No. LinkedIn has no setting or filter for reposted jobs, not even with Premium. You can dismiss them one by one with the ✕, or use a browser extension that hides them for you.

What does Reposted mean on a LinkedIn job?

The company posted the same job again, usually because it is still open. The job can be weeks old and may already have many applicants, even though it shows up as new.

Why do reposted jobs matter if I want to apply early?

They look fresh but are often old jobs with many applicants. Every one takes a place on your screen that a really new job could use.

Does EarlyApply hide reposted jobs for free?

Yes. The Reposted switch in the EarlyApply filter bar is free, needs no account, and hides every reposted job on every search.

How can I tell if a LinkedIn job was reposted?

The job card says "Reposted X days ago" where the posted time is normally shown.

Does a reposted job mean the company isn't hiring?

Not usually. A job is often posted again because the position is still open. But it can already be weeks old with a long line of applicants, so it is a poor bet if you want to apply early.
