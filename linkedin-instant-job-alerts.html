You set up a LinkedIn job alert, and the email arrives the next morning. By then the job already has a hundred applicants. Here's why LinkedIn's alerts are slow, how to get the most out of them, and how to hear about a new job within a few minutes of it going up.

Short answer: LinkedIn has no instant job alert. Its alerts come as a daily or weekly email, or an app notification it times itself. To hear within minutes, you need something that checks your search every few minutes, like the free Job Radar in EarlyApply.

Set up LinkedIn's own alert properly

It's still worth having, as a backup:

  1. Search with the exact filters you want

    Keywords, location, remote or on-site, experience level. The alert sends whatever this search finds, so a tight search means fewer useless emails.

  2. Turn on "Set alert"

    At the top of the results, switch on Set alert (or Job alert).

  3. Choose the fastest delivery

    In your job alert settings, pick Daily rather than weekly, and turn on notifications in the LinkedIn app as well as email.

Questions

Why are my LinkedIn job alerts late?

LinkedIn collects new jobs and sends them in batches, usually as a daily or weekly email. App notifications can be faster, but LinkedIn decides when to send them, so a job can be hours old when you hear about it.

Can LinkedIn send job alerts instantly?

Not reliably. There is no "instant" option for job alerts. To hear about a job within minutes, something has to check your search every few minutes, such as a browser extension like EarlyApply's Job Radar.

Does Job Radar work when my computer is off?

No. Job Radar runs in your own Chrome or Edge, so the computer and browser need to be on. It keeps working while you use other apps or tabs.

Is checking LinkedIn every few minutes safe?

EarlyApply checks no faster than every 3 minutes, in your own LinkedIn tab, like reloading the page yourself. It never applies, clicks or sends messages for you.
