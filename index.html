Features

Everything you need to be early. Nothing that applies for you.

The first applicants get read first. EarlyApply puts the newest jobs in front of you and keeps the rest out of the way.

See LinkedIn jobs posted in the last hour first

One click filters your search to jobs posted in the last 15 minutes, 30 minutes or a few hours, sorted newest first.

  • Time filters LinkedIn doesn't offer, right on the page
  • Fresh jobs are highlighted with how long ago they were posted
  • Works on LinkedIn's classic and AI-powered search

Job alerts the moment a job is posted

Turn it on for any search and keep working. Job Radar checks it every few minutes and tells you the minute something new appears.

  • Desktop alert and a soft chime, even in other apps
  • The toolbar icon lights up with the number of new jobs
  • Remembers your search, even after Chrome restarts

Hide companies and job titles you don't want

Block a company once and it never shows up in your searches again. Block job titles by word: a fresher can hide "Senior" and "Lead", a developer can hide "Sales" or "BPO".

  • One click "Hide company" on any job card
  • Also hides promoted, reposted and too-old jobs, and staffing agencies
  • "N hidden · show" lets you peek at what was filtered

Saved answers and a daily goal

Notice period, salary, a short intro: keep your usual answers and copy them in one click beside LinkedIn's Easy Apply form.

  • A card shows the answers that fit each question
  • Set a daily goal and keep your streak going
  • You stay in control: EarlyApply never fills or submits forms

How it works

Set up in under a minute.

  1. Add EarlyApply to Chrome

    Free from the Chrome Web Store. No account, no sign-up.

  2. Search on LinkedIn Jobs

    The EarlyApply bar appears above the results. Pick 15 minutes and hide the noise.

  3. Turn on Job Radar

    Get an alert when a new job lands, open it, and be one of the first to apply.

Private by design

Your job search stays yours.
