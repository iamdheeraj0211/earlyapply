LinkedIn moved everyone to its AI-powered job search and took away the "Switch to classic job search" button. Most people miss two things: sorting by Most recent, and seeing only the jobs posted in the last hour. Here is what changed, and the workaround that still works.

Short answer: there is no switch back while you're logged in. The closest thing is LinkedIn's logged-out job list: open linkedin.com/jobs/search in a private window, search, and add &sortBy=DD (newest first) and &f_TPR=r3600 (last hour) to the address. Sign in only when you apply.

The workaround: LinkedIn's logged-out job list

  1. Open a private window

    Ctrl+Shift+N in Chrome, Ctrl+Shift+P in Edge (Cmd on a Mac). You stay logged in in your normal window.

  2. Search on linkedin.com/jobs/search

    Type your job title and location as usual. If a sign-in pop-up appears, close it.

  3. Add newest first and a time window to the address

    Click the address bar and add these two settings at the end, then press Enter:

    https://www.linkedin.com/jobs/search?keywords=data%20analyst&location=India&sortBy=DD&f_TPR=r3600

    sortBy=DD puts the newest job on top. f_TPR=r3600 keeps only the last hour (3,600 seconds). See all the f_TPR codes.

  4. Use the filters it shows

    Logged out, LinkedIn shows Past 24 hours, Company, Easy Apply and Under 10 applicants. That last one is great for applying early.

  5. Bookmark it, sign in to apply

    Save the address as a bookmark and open it in a private window. When a job looks right, click Apply and sign in.

Good to know

  • Fewer filters logged out. Experience level, remote and job type menus are hidden. Put the most important words in your keywords instead.
  • No job alerts or saved searches without signing in. For alerts, see how to get job alerts within minutes.
  • Promoted and reposted jobs still show up. See how to remove promoted and reposted jobs.
  • LinkedIn can change this any time. The logged-out list is LinkedIn's own public page, but it is not an official "classic mode". We'll update this guide if it stops working.

Questions

Can I switch back to classic job search on LinkedIn?

Not while logged in. LinkedIn removed the Switch to classic job search button, and the old address now opens the AI-powered search. The closest thing is LinkedIn's logged-out job list: open linkedin.com/jobs/search in a private window.

Why can't I sort LinkedIn jobs by Most recent anymore?

LinkedIn's AI-powered search has no sort option. Results are ordered by relevance only. Logged out, adding sortBy=DD to the search address still sorts the list newest first.

Does the f_TPR last-hour trick still work?

Only when you are logged out. Logged in, the AI-powered search treats any time filter under 24 hours as 24 hours. Logged out, f_TPR=r3600 still shows only jobs from the last hour.

Can I apply to jobs from the logged-out list?

Yes. Open the job and sign in when you click Apply or Easy Apply. If LinkedIn doesn't return you to the job, search for it again while logged in.
